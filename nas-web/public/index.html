    Tornado account Checking…

    Chrome being signed in does not sign in the NAS. Copy two Tornado cookies and paste them here.

    1. Open Tornado in Chrome and stay signed in. Use Open Tornado if you need the tab.
    2. Right-click the page → Inspect → Application → Cookies → https://www4.tornadomovies.co (not Shared storage).
    3. Copy the Value for _identity-frontend1 and advanced-frontend. Skip Google cookies such as _GRECAPTCHA.
    4. Paste one line below as _identity-frontend1=…; advanced-frontend=…, then Save cookie.
